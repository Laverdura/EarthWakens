Angels

Angels are what make Existence plausible. They are the energy that unites everything together. They are everything, and they exist within the Spiritual Realm.

No deity directs the angels in this story. They take no orders. They are the energy of Existence, uniting everything within that realm.

Living in Harmony

The animals of the Living World were not without intelligence. They could learn, remember, solve problems, and act upon their surroundings. Instinct was part of their lives, but intelligence belonged there too.

In this story, the animals existed in harmony with the Spiritual Realm without questioning that existence. They had not experienced the encounter that would change the Earth Spirit’s understanding of itself.

Wanting to Become

Earth’s living forms developed across billions of years. The Earth Spirit’s awareness grew gradually. Becoming cognizant of its own existence did not give it an immediate understanding of the Spiritual Realm. It was only beginning to recognize what had always been there.

As that awareness grew, the Earth Spirit recognized infinity beyond the limits of its physical living form. It wanted more than to exist within the Spiritual Realm. It wanted to become the Spiritual Realm itself.

Even the Angels Could Not Tell

Even the angels could not tell the difference between what they had always known themselves to be, the Spiritual Realm, and the Earth Spirit. All existed as one. None could distinguish one from another.

That union was serene and tranquil, beyond anything the Earth Spirit had known. Its magnificence exceeded every word that might describe it. Having experienced it, the Earth Spirit wanted to remain.

The Earth Spirit and the Spiritual Realm are the two counterparts in this story. Neither has a gender. Their union is an encounter with Existence itself.

Yet the Earth Spirit remained alive. Its physical living form still belonged to the Living World, where birth, death, and renewal continued. It had come to know timeless Existence while remaining bound to a form that could not live forever.

That is the conflict the Earth Spirit carries forward.
