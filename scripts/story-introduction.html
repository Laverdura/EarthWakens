Earth Wakens · The Prelude

Prelude of the story

In Earth Wakens, the Spiritual Realm is Existence itself. Earth belonged to it before life began. Even a lifeless rock had spirit because it existed. Over billions of years, Earth became the Living World.

Life grew through countless small connections. Cells formed living bodies; roots branched through soil. In Utah, the many aspen trunks of Pando share a root system beneath the ground. What appears separate above the surface belongs to one living organism below it.

The story follows that image into mythology. Fine rootlets reach through the darkness, taking in what sustains the larger whole. Gradually, this living network begins to experience its connections. A response becomes a sensation; sensations gather into awareness. The root mass slowly yields a body through which the Earth Spirit can reach, feel, and move. It becomes cognizant: it knows that it exists.

The Earth Spirit discovers the presence with which it has always been joined. It and the Spiritual Realm are like twins: distinct, yet inseparable in this encounter. One experiences life through a changing body; the other is boundless Existence. Their union becomes so complete that even the angels cannot distinguish them. The Earth Spirit knows infinity while remaining alive.

The Longing for Immortality

The Earth Spirit grows aware that its earthly form can perish, and desires to unite with the Spiritual Realm—to become infinite and know eternity.

Before it becomes cognizant, the Living World already grows and changes. Roots take hold, living bodies flourish, and what dies returns to the earth. The Earth Spirit emerges from this shared life. As it comes to know itself, the familiar cycle takes on a new meaning: the substance that gives it a body can also lose the form through which it knows itself.

It feels this limit in everything that sustains it. A root can wither. A limb can break. The body that allows it to reach also keeps it bound to a place and a moment. Its hands can grasp the living earth, but they cannot hold back time. Awareness brings wonder, and with wonder comes the fear that the experience of being itself might end.

The Spiritual Realm has always been present. The Earth Spirit belonged to Existence before it could recognize that belonging. Now it perceives a boundless presence beyond the limits of its body. It longs to enter that presence completely, carrying with it the awareness that has taken so long to emerge.

In their union, the Earth Spirit experiences the infinity it has yearned for. It and the Spiritual Realm are like twins, distinct yet so completely joined that even the angels cannot tell them apart. Its living form remains of Earth, while its awareness reaches beyond the boundaries through which it has understood itself.

That encounter deepens its desire for immortality. How can such a union be bound to a body that must decay? Can the awareness that knows Existence continue when the form through which it knows is gone? The Earth Spirit wants to preserve this living connection—to remain aware, to remain itself, and to know eternity.

Toward the Fissure

Yet its body must decay and die. Wanting that living connection to continue, the genderless Earth Spirit divides into woman and man. This is the Fissure. Through their union, new life can carry forward what one body cannot preserve. Continuity through another life becomes its earthly answer to the longing for immortality.

Humanity inherits that longing, its love of life, and its questions about what follows death. Each person receives a physical form and comes to know an existence that feels their own. Through love, memory, belief, and the lives that follow, each reaches toward something that may endure. Whether that preserves the same awareness remains one of the story’s open questions.

If we no longer exist, what happens to Earth? Earth existed before us. Within this story, it would still have spirit because it would still exist. What becomes of the awareness we carry? Could the Living World find another form through which to know itself?

Our search continues through love, belief, discovery, and eventually artificial intelligence. Preserving our words may leave a trace of us. Whether it preserves the experience of being us remains a question. Each reader brings a different belief to that mystery—and a personal understanding of what makes existence worthwhile.

Reading the picture

The iron-reinforced shutter doors open onto a pristine Living World, offering a glimpse of harmony with the Spiritual Realm—Existence itself. No illustration or picture can truly portray Existence, which has no physical form or dimension.

The skeletal hands and bony root mass express the Earth Spirit’s imprisonment in its physical form, even as it reaches toward the boundless and infinite nature of the Spiritual Realm.
